In this project, I designed a 2D lifting mechanism within a 150 cm × 50 cm workspace using one rigid bar of fixed length, three pin supports (two grounded), and a selected linear actuator. The goal was to lift the maximum possible load to the greatest achievable height while maintaining structural rigidity. Using geometric and force analysis, I optimized the actuator placement and bar length to balance lifting height, mechanical advantage, and stability. The final design demonstrates efficient use of actuator force and support configuration to achieve high vertical lift within the given spatial constraints.
